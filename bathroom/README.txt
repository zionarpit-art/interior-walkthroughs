Bathroom Style Viewer

Open: double-click index.html (Chrome, Edge, Safari or Firefox; internet needed once for three.js/Spark from the CDN).
Share: drag this whole folder onto https://app.netlify.com/drop  or push it to a GitHub repo and enable Pages.

Controls: drag = look/orbit, scroll or pinch = zoom, tap a surface = next finish ripples out from there,
buttons = pick a finish, Tour = automatic camera path, keys 1/2 or Space also switch.

Detail: the page opens on the first real render (photo) while the HD 3D version of the shown finish
downloads (badge top-right shows HD %); the tour starts moving once it is in, so visitors never see
the light version smear. Then the other photos and the other finish load in the background.
Phones get HD too (unless the browser reports under 4 GB memory).
Photo stops: when the tour pauses, or you pick a view (Bathtub, Vanity, Shower, Toilet), the real Cycles render
fades in. Clicking still ripples the next finish across the photo. Drag to leave the photo.
Add ?lite to the link to skip HD on a slow device, or ?hd to force it.
